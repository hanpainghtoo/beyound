## Design Context

### Users
Finance operators manage the platform's payment-receiving bank accounts and digital wallets. They need fast, confident account maintenance with clear payment-channel details and low-friction editing.

### Brand Personality
Operational, trustworthy, and clear. The interface should evoke confidence and control without adding visual noise.

### Aesthetic Direction
Match the existing ZayOS platform console exactly: dark navy surfaces, sky-blue accents, compact data-dense layouts, shadcn-style controls, and modal CRUD workflows.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize scanability for finance operations: clear labels, masked sensitive values, and strong status badges.
- Reuse existing platform-console components, spacing, roles, and interaction patterns rather than introducing a parallel design system.
- Make conditional account-type fields explicit and prevent invalid submissions before they reach the API.
- Keep destructive actions reversible in presentation: call soft deletion “Deactivate” and make the inactive state visible.
- Show loading, empty, error, and success states with the same calm, direct language as the rest of the console.
