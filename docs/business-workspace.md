# Business Workspace: Page-by-Page Design

## 1. Dashboard Overview
**Purpose:** Summary of key activities and system status.

**Sections:**
- **Stats Summary Cards:** Today's Conversations, Active CSRs, Pending Tickets, Channel Status.
- **Charts:** Daily Conversation Trend, Channel Usage Breakdown, Response Time Graph.
- **CSR Activity Snapshot:** Table of csr stats.
- **System Notices:** Alerts for channel issues or plan limits.

**Actions:** Navigate modules, filter analytics, quick fixes.

---

## 2. Team Management
**Purpose:** Manage sellers, csrs, supervisors, and business operators.

**Sections:**
- **Team Member List Table:** Profile, Email, Role, Status, Online Status, Chats Today, Actions.
- **Bulk Actions:** Activate, deactivate, delete.
- **Filters & Search:** By name, email, role, status.
- **Add/Edit Team Member Form:** Name, Email, Phone, Role, Password, Status.
- **Role & Permission Settings:** Custom roles, permission toggles.
- **Quick Actions:** Add team member, download CSV, productivity stats.

**Access:** Business admin/Supervisor only.

---

## 3. Channel Management
**Purpose:** Connect and configure messaging channels.

**Sections:**
- **Connected Channels List:** Logo, Name, Status, Actions.
- **Add New Channel:** Wizard for supported platforms.
- **Channel Settings Panel:** Display name, greeting, auto-reply, assignment, notifications.
- **Channel Analytics Snapshot:** Message stats.

**Actions:** Connect, configure, reconnect, remove channels.

**Access:** Business admin/Supervisor only.

---

## 4. Canned Responses Management
**Purpose:** Manage pre-written replies.

**Sections:**
- **Canned Responses List:** Shortcut, Title, Preview, Tags, Visibility, Actions.
- **Filters & Search:** By keyword, tag, visibility, creator.
- **Add/Edit Modal:** Title, Shortcut, Content, Tags, Visibility.
- **Tag Management:** Create, edit, delete tags.
- **Bulk Actions:** Delete, change visibility, assign tags.

**Access:** Admin/Supervisor manage; csrs limited.

---

## 5. Product & Service Catalog
**Purpose:** Manage products/services for chat orders.

**Sections:**
- **Product/Service List Table:** Image, Name, SKU, Type, Price, Stock, Status, Actions.
- **Filters & Search:** By name, SKU, type, status, category, price.
- **Add/Edit Modal:** Name, Type, SKU, Description, Image, Price, Stock, Category, Status.
- **Category Management:** Create/edit/delete categories.
- **Bulk Actions:** Update status, delete, assign category, export CSV.
- **Integration:** Sync with e-commerce platforms.

**Access:** Admin/Supervisor manage; csrs view only.

---

## 6. Orders & Invoice Settings
**Purpose:** Configure order/invoice rules and integrations.

**Sections:**
- **Invoice Configuration:** Prefix, format, tax, currency, terms, footer.
- **Delivery & Fees Setup:** Rules by region, payment, amount, type.
- **Payment Method Configuration:** Enable/disable COD, online, bank transfer.
- **Order Lifecycle Configuration:** Statuses for New, Confirmed, Packed, Out for delivery, Delivered, COD collected, Cancelled, Returned.
- **Delivery Assignment Rules:** Manual delivery assignment first; delivery partner integrations later.
- **COD Ledger Settings:** Track pending collection, collected, failed collection, and reconciled collection.
- **Partial Payment Rules:** Support deposits, split payment, bank transfer plus COD, and remaining balance.
- **Integration:** External invoice systems, API setup.
- **Order Routing Rules:** Auto-assign by department, csr, tags.
- **Invoice Template Selection:** Choose/customize templates.

**Access:** Admin only; Supervisors view-only.

---

## 7. Chat Reports & Analytics
**Purpose:** Monitor messaging volume, csr performance, channel activity.

**Sections:**
- **Filter Panel:** Date, channel, csr, status, export.
- **Summary KPIs:** Conversations, response time, messages per chat, active csrs.
- **Charts:** Conversation volume, csr performance, channel distribution.
- **CSR Performance Table:** Conversations, reply time, handle time, CSAT, status.
- **CSR Productivity Scoreboard:** Conversations handled, first response time, orders created, conversion rate, SLA breaches.
- **SLA Dashboard:** Unread, hot leads, overdue conversations, SLA trend.
- **Conversation Heatmap:** Peak chat hours.
- **Report Templates:** Predefined exports, scheduling.

**Access:** Admin/Supervisor only.

---

## 8. General Settings
**Purpose:** Manage account preferences and branding.

**Sections:**
- **Company Profile & Branding:** Name, logo, email, phone, website, description.
- **Locale & Regional Settings:** Language, timezone, date/currency/number format.
- **Notification Preferences:** Types, channels, recipients.
- **Account Contact Info:** Primary contact, support email, mobile, role.
- **Billing Information:** Plan, expiry, quotas, manage billing.

**Access:** Admin edit; Supervisors view-only.

---

## 9. Roles & Permissions
**Purpose:** Define user access and permissions.

**Sections:**
- **Predefined Roles:** Admin, Supervisor, CSR.
- **Custom Role Management:** Create, edit, delete roles.
- **Permission Matrix:** Toggle access per module and action.
- **Assign Users to Roles:** Batch assign, filter by role/status.
- **Role Hierarchy:** Set priority levels.

**Access:** Admin only.

---

## 10. Audit Logs
**Purpose:** Track user actions and changes.

**Sections:**
- **Filters & Search:** Date, user, action, module, keyword.
- **Audit Log Table:** Timestamp, user, action, module, details, IP.
- **Expandable Row:** View before/after changes.
- **Export Logs:** Download CSV/JSON, schedule exports.
- **Settings:** Enable/disable logging, retention, anonymize IPs.

**Access:** Admin and users with permission.

## 11. Customer 360 Timeline
**Purpose:** Provide one customer truth layer for support, sales, payments, calls, and future AI.

**Sections:**
- **Unified Timeline:** Chats, orders, order status changes, payments, calls, notes, complaints, tags.
- **Timeline Filters:** Event type, channel, date, csr, order, payment state.
- **Customer Summary:** Current open conversations, recent orders, COD status, complaint risk, VIP/repeat buyer tags.
- **Manual Events:** Add note, log complaint, record payment, log call.
- **AI-Ready Context:** Timeline data can feed summaries, reply suggestions, and customer risk scoring.

**Access:** Admin/Supervisor full view; csrs see assigned/customer-relevant timeline.
